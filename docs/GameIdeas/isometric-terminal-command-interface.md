# Idea: Isometric Terminal as Full Command Interface

## The Idea
The terminal inside the control tower is a full isometric base management interface — like Clash of Clans viewed from above. It is the only place in the game where you can see your entire base at once. A player must be physically standing at it to use it.

## What You Can Do From the Terminal
- Pan and zoom across the full drone coverage area
- Assess wall tiers and damage states at a glance
- Issue repair orders — "repair all walls" dispatches bots automatically
- Use manual repair tool to selectively repair priority infrastructure when resources are limited
- Upgrade walls and infrastructure directly without running to them
- Monitor incoming wave vectors before ground players see anything
- Track manufacturing throughput and production bottlenecks

## The Co-op Commander Role
- Ground players see only what's in front of them
- The commander sees everything but can't act physically
- Full squad split: one commander calling positions and issuing orders, three players executing
- The commander is playing a different game — RTS while teammates play a shooter
- Neither role is complete without the other

## Solo Play Cost
- You have to physically run to the terminal to use it
- Check the overview, assess, run to the problem, fix it, run back
- Good players develop a rhythm of when to be at the terminal vs on the ground

## Why the Terminal Must Be Physical
- If it's accessible anywhere it becomes a passive menu — no strategic cost
- Being at the terminal means you're not shooting
- Losing the terminal during a wave means losing command at the worst moment
- The physical location makes the control tower worth defending

## Open Questions
- Can a second terminal be built elsewhere sharing the same drone network?
- Does the terminal have a boot-up time after the tower takes damage?
