# Idea: Dual Perspective — First Person + Isometric Terminal

## The Idea
The game is primarily first-person. You move, fight, build, and repair in first person. The isometric view is not a camera toggle — it's a physical terminal inside the control tower that a player has to be standing at to use. No building or direct interaction from isometric — that's purely a command and monitoring interface.

## First Person
- Default mode for all players at all times
- Movement, combat, building placement, repairs, crafting all done in first person
- What you see is limited to your immediate surroundings
- During waves: you're in the chaos, responding to what you can directly observe

## Isometric Terminal
- Only accessible by standing at the terminal inside the control tower
- Shows everything within drone coverage as an overhead view
- Pan and zoom freely within coverage area
- Issue repair orders, upgrade walls, monitor throughput — all from here
- See incoming wave vectors before ground players know what's coming
- Cannot directly shoot, build, or interact with the physical world from this view

## The Co-op Split
- One player at the terminal = commander role
- Everyone else on the ground = execution role
- Commander has full situational awareness, zero physical agency
- Ground players have physical agency, limited information
- Neither role works without the other in a full squad

## Solo Play
- You physically run to the terminal, check the overview, run back to act on it
- The cost of the run is the strategic tension — is it worth leaving the fight to get better information?
- Good players develop a rhythm for when to be at the terminal vs on the ground

## Why Not a Camera Toggle
- A toggle has no cost — you'd just be in isometric all the time
- Physical location creates a real strategic tradeoff
- Makes the control tower worth defending — losing it means losing command
- Diegetic: you're not a god looking down, you're a worker at a terminal

## Open Questions
- Is there any HUD overlay in first person that shows minimal info from the terminal (like a minimap)?
- Can the terminal be used during a wave or does it have some kind of override/lockout?
- Does the commander have any voice/ping system to communicate positions to ground players?
