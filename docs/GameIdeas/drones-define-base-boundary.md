# Idea: Drones Define Base Boundary — No Physical Barriers

## The Idea
Instead of physical walls or an arbitrary radius defining where you can build, the control tower deploys drones that patrol the surrounding area. The edge of their coverage pattern is the edge of your buildable zone. Upgrade the drones, expand the base.

## Why This Works
- The boundary is always visually obvious — drones are physical objects you can see
- No invisible walls or grid lines the player has to mentally track
- Coverage scales with investment, not placement — the factory layout is never constrained by tower position
- More drones = wider patrol pattern = bigger base. Simple and legible.

## What Upgrading Does
- More drones deployed
- Wider patrol radius per drone
- Denser coverage pattern
- Drone upgrades are earned through wave survival — minor modules from supplemental waves, major modules from biome boss waves

## Why Not Physical Barriers
- Physical barriers force factory layout decisions around the barrier, not around production logic
- Arbitrary radius with dead zones creates invisible gotcha moments
- Drone coverage is self-explanatory — if you can see a drone flying there, you can build there

## Open Questions

- Can drones be shot down during waves, temporarily shrinking the buildable zone?
- Do drones provide any combat assistance or are they purely surveillance/coverage?
