# Idea: Control Tower — Four Concentric Rings

## The Idea
The control tower isn't just a building — it's the operational center of the entire base. It emits four distinct zones of influence that scale outward as you upgrade it. You can build anywhere on the map but what your base can actually do is defined by these rings.

## The Four Rings

### Ring 1 — Zone of Influence (Outermost)
- Creatures cannot spawn inside this zone outside of active waves
- Your safe working area between waves
- Waves temporarily override this — the ecosystem is pushing hard enough to break through
- Largest radius of the four

### Ring 2 — Terminal Coverage
- What the commander sees from the isometric interface
- Drones patrol this zone physically
- Everything inside is visible, manageable, and actionable from the terminal

### Ring 3 — Power Transmission
- Machines inside this range are active and producing
- Machines outside it are built but dormant
- Power relays extend this range in chosen directions — strategic investment
- Extended power lines are physical infrastructure that can be destroyed

### Ring 4 — Bot Repair Radius (Innermost)
- Automated repair operates inside this zone
- Outside this zone damage requires a player to physically run to it and fix it manually
- Smallest radius — the core base is maintained, the frontier is your responsibility

## All Four Scale With Tower Upgrades
Each control tower upgrade pushes all four rings outward — not necessarily at the same rate. You might invest in power range separately from bot radius depending on how the upgrade tree is structured.

## Open Questions
