# Idea: Tower Floors as Hard Drive Equivalents

## The Idea
Inspired by Satisfactory's hard drive system. Tower contract floors contain data chips, schematics, and corrupted S.L.O.P. logs that function as discoverable research — alternate recipes, new buildable blueprints, production optimizations. Finding them rewards thorough exploration over speed running.

## Two Distinct Tower Reward Layers

### Contract Completion Reward
- The guaranteed unlock at the end of a contract
- The main blueprint or recipe the contract was designed to yield
- Tied directly to the contract progression tree
- Always known in advance from the contract board intel

### Floor Discoveries
- Optional finds while clearing floors
- Data chips, schematics, corrupted logs scattered through the building
- Yield alternate recipes — different ways to produce existing items, more efficient production chains
- Rewards players who thoroughly clear vs players who rush to completion
- A contract you've already completed might still have undiscovered floors worth revisiting

## Why This Works
- Separates guaranteed progression from exploration rewards
- Gives the Tower replay value beyond just the next contract tier
- Alternate recipes are meaningful factory upgrades without being required — you can play without them but they make things more efficient
- Fits the world — these buildings had research, data, and proprietary processes inside them before the collapse

## Relation to the Scanner
- The resource scanner (unlocked via control tower upgrades) tells you where resource nodes are on the map
- The Tower discovery system is the knowledge equivalent — it tells your factory new ways to use what it already has
- Two different kinds of discovery: where to find things vs how to process them better

## Open Questions
- Are floor discoveries visible on the isometric terminal map or only discoverable in first person?
- Do alternate recipes require specific machines to use or do they slot into existing ones?
- Is there a research interface where you select which alternate recipe to unlock, like Satisfactory's hard drive choice?
