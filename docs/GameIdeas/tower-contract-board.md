# Idea: Tower Contract Board

## The Idea
The Tower isn't a single persistent building or a randomized rotation. It's a contract progression tree. Management issues contracts and you pick the order you do them in, but you can't skip tiers.

## How It Works
- At any given time a set of available contracts are shown on a board
- Each contract lists known intel: threat level, suspected enemy types, guaranteed loot, recommended player count
- Completing contracts unlocks the next tier — harder buildings, better rewards
- The contract tree has a narrative spine — Management knows exactly what they want recovered and why

## Why It's Better Than Alternatives
- **Randomized rotation**: High replayability but no progression or narrative investment
- **Single persistent tower**: Strong narrative but no variety
- **Contract board**: Player agency over order without randomness. Strategic choice not a dice roll

## Open Questions
- How many contracts per tier?
- Do failed contracts stay available or rotate out?
- Is the full tree visible from the start or does it reveal as you progress?
